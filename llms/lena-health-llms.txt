# Lena Health - AI-Powered Care Navigation

> Lena Health combines AI with human navigators to improve patient outcomes through care coordination and navigation services.

## About Lena Health

Lena Health is a healthcare technology company based in Houston, Texas, founded in the world's largest medical center (Texas Medical Center). We believe nobody should have to navigate care alone.

Our platform, LenaConnect, uses AI to handle routine tasks like outreach calls, appointment coordination, hold times, and documentation—so human navigators can focus on what matters: building trust with patients and providing personalized care.

## What We Do

### Managed Care Services
We provide fully managed care navigation services for:
- **Chronic Care Management (CCM)**: Ongoing support for patients with chronic conditions
- **Transitional Care Management (TCM)**: Post-discharge navigation to reduce readmissions
- **Rising-Risk Care Management**: Proactive intervention for at-risk patients
- **Annual Wellness Visits**: Preventive care scheduling and follow-up

### LenaConnect Platform
Our AI platform handles:
- Routine outreach calls and patient contact
- Appointment scheduling and coordination
- Managing hold times with payers and providers
- Automatic documentation and EHR integration
- Resource coordination and referral management

## Proven Outcomes

Based on retrospective matched-cohort studies:
- **29%** reduction in unplanned readmissions
- **42%** reduction in ED visits
- **$3,116** saved per patient within 180 days
- **11x** return on investment
